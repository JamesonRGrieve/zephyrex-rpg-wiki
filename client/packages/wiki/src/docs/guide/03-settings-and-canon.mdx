# Settings and canon

A setting is the universe a campaign is played in. It holds the things everyone there would know:
its canon.

## Canon

A record with a setting and no campaign is canon. On the setting's own pages (the **Its canon**
link under the setting on /w) you list and write its canon exactly as you would a campaign's
records. Every campaign in the setting sees that canon, read-only, beside its own records: on a
campaign's Places page, for example, the setting's places are listed under *Places of the
setting*.

A record's setting, like its campaign, is fixed once it is set. Moving a record means copying it
and deleting the old one.

## Slugs

Each setting has a slug, a stable short name such as `reality`. It is made from the name when you
don't give one, numbered past any already taken, and never changes afterwards. Importers and the
VTT's game systems find a setting by its slug.

## Eras

A setting can span several eras, the way Reality spans every period of history. Tag canon records
and campaigns with an **era** (a tag of kind `era`):

- a campaign with an era sees its era's canon and the canon that has no era;
- it never sees another era's canon;
- a campaign with no era sees all of it.

The rule is the same on both editions (`contract/era-cases.json`).

## Sections from other products

An app that composes the wiki can add its own sections to each setting. The VTT adds the
setting's game systems this way. They appear under the setting on /w and on its canon pages.
