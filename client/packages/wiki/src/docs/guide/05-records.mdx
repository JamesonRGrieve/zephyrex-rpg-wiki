# Records

Places, people, organisations, items, journals and hooks share one kind of page.

## The list

Each kind's page lists the campaign's records, with a field to add one by name and kind. Places
and organisations also show as a tree, each under the one it sits in or answers to. Delete has an
**Undo**: the record is hidden at once and deleted a few seconds later unless you take it back.

## A record's page

- **The entry**: what the record is, as rich text. Its plain-text copy is kept as the record's
  description, for search and for the AI.
- **Picture**: an `https` link, shown with no referrer. Data URLs are refused.
- **Origin**: homebrew, official or adapted, with its source.
- **Kind**: from the contract's list for that type (a place is a city, a building, a room...).
- **Where**: a person's location, a place's parent, an organisation's parent.
- **Aliases and keywords**: other names the record goes by, and words that bring it up in the
  AI's lore.
- **Voice** (people): how the AI plays them, in SillyTavern's parts: personality, speech, first
  message, examples. A person can also have a TTS voice for spoken lines.
- **Lore activation**: when the AI should bring the record into its context.
- **Posts**: extra rich-text sections pinned to the record.
- **Inventory** (people and places): what they carry or hold.

## Saving

Every change is checked against the version you loaded. If someone else changed the record in the
meantime, your change is refused and the page says so, with their version, instead of quietly
overwriting it.

## Templates

A record can be a template: a stock NPC or a standard room, copied into campaigns. Templates
never count as facts of the world they sit in.
