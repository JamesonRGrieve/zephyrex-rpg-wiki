# The plot board

Hooks are a campaign's quests, mysteries, rumours and threads. The **Plot board** shows them by
state.

## Requirements

A hook can require others: "find the shard" before "fix the waystone". Requirements are
expressions with **all** and **any**, and can include a check at the table ("a psyker or a scholar
hears the ghost"). The wiki refuses a requirement that would make a loop.

## States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Can happen now | Everything it needs has happened |
| Blocked | Something it needs hasn't happened yet; its excuse says why |
| Needs a check at the table | What's left is a roll the group has to make |
| Happened | Resolved, with the session it was resolved in |

**Mark as happened** and **Mark as not happened** move a hook between them.

## Who is on it

A hook names who gave it, and which people and organisations are on it, with their progress. Its
resolution can change relations and dispositions (see
[Tags, relations and properties](?path=/docs/wiki-guide-6-tags-relations-and-properties--docs)).
