# Troubleshooting

**A section I expected isn't there.** The page shows a feature only when the back end lists the
extension that serves it. Check `GET /v1/extension` (or `/api/v1/extension` on lite).

**"Someone else changed it".** The record changed after you opened it. The page shows the current
version; make your change again on top of it.

**A list says it could not load.** The back end refused or didn't answer. **Try again** reloads
it; the server's log has the reason.

**An importer says the setting doesn't exist.** Make it first with `POST /v1/setting`, then pass
its slug.

**An import says UNPLAYABLE.** A scenario has no framing, people with no kind, or player
characters with no sheet. Run `python -m scenario_repair` to see what's wrong, then
`--apply` to mend what it can.

**A campaign doesn't show a setting's records.** Check their eras. A campaign with an era sees only
canon of its era and canon with none
([Settings and canon](?path=/docs/wiki-guide-3-settings-and-canon--docs)).

**A picture won't save.** Pictures are `https` links. Data URLs and plain `http` are refused.
